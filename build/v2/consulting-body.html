A Few Things You Might Be Wondering.

Is this only about marketing?

No. While marketing and creative development are among our strongest areas, our consulting covers business planning, operations, logistics, sales, pricing, technology, product development, growth, and other practical business challenges.

I’ve never started a business before. Can you help?

Absolutely. We can help you organize your idea, understand the decisions ahead, explore your options, and establish priorities for getting started. You don’t need to have everything figured out before our first meeting.

What if I don’t know exactly what my business needs?

That’s perfectly fine. Many consultations begin with a conversation about where the business stands and what the owner wants to achieve. Together, we can identify the areas worth exploring.

Do you need to research my business before we meet?

It depends on the consultation. A focused guidance session may only require a brief questionnaire. More complex strategic engagements include discovery, independent research, and preparation before the main consultation.

Will you give me a business plan?

We’ll provide recommendations and next steps appropriate to your selected package. More comprehensive business plans, financial models, or detailed implementation documents can be scoped separately.

Can you help implement your recommendations?

Yes. Highway 19 Media offers website development, branding, advertising, video production, and other creative and marketing services. Where a project requires specialized expertise outside our services, we can help identify what kind of professional support may be needed.

Can we meet in person?

Yes. We’re based in Spring Hill, Florida, and can arrange local meetings at your business or another suitable location. Remote consultations are also available.

Can we schedule regular consultations?

Yes. Weekly, monthly, and quarterly advisory arrangements are available. We’ll determine the frequency and scope based on your goals and the level of support you need.

What if I need legal, accounting, or tax advice?

We can help you organize your business questions and understand the operational decisions involved, but legal, tax, accounting, and regulated financial matters should be handled by appropriately qualified professionals.

Will I be working directly with the consultant?

Yes. Your consultations are personally conducted by the founder of Highway 19 Media, bringing decades of hands-on business, marketing, and creative experience to the conversation.

Can you guarantee business growth?

No responsible consultant can guarantee a particular outcome. Our role is to help you understand your options, make informed decisions, identify opportunities, and develop practical strategies that support your business goals.
