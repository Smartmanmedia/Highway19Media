What we collect, and why

What you hand us on the contact form, the ordinary server records every website keeps and, only if you accept it, visit statistics from Google Analytics.

What you type into the form

The contact form asks for your name and email, and optionally your website, phone number, what you are interested in, how you heard about us, and any notes. We use it for one thing: to read what you sent and reply to it. We do not add you to a mailing list from it.

Ordinary server records

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like every web host, it records requests — the page asked for, the time, the browser, and the IP address it came from — to serve the site and to keep it from being attacked. We do not use those records to build a picture of you, and we do not combine them with anything from the form.

Visit statistics, only if you say yes

When you first visit, a banner asks whether we may use Google Analytics. If you accept, it records how you arrived, which pages you read, roughly where you are (city level, from your IP address) and what kind of device and browser you use, and it sets its own cookies to tell a returning visitor from a new one. If you click a Contact Us button or send the contact form, that is counted too, so we know which pages bring enquiries. It does not receive what you type into the form.

If you decline, or your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, Google Analytics is never loaded at all. You can change your answer at any time with the Cookie choices link at the bottom of every page. The details are in the Cookie Policy.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts, no passwords — there is nothing to log in to.
  • No payments are taken on this site.
  • No advertising pixel or advertising cookie is running here today. If that changes, this page and the Cookie Policy will say so first and the banner will ask you again.
  • We do not knowingly collect anything from children under 13.

Who else touches it

Four companies, each doing a specific job, none of them buying your details from us.

  • Web3Forms receives the contact form when you press send and passes it to our inbox. It sees what is in the form.
  • Google (Gmail) is where that email lands and is read.
  • Cloudflare serves the pages and keeps the server records described above.
  • Google (Analytics) receives the visit statistics described above, and only if you accepted them. Google’s own use of that data is covered by its privacy policy.

Beyond those four, nobody. We do not sell, rent or trade personal information, and we do not share it for anyone else's advertising.

How long we keep it

Enquiry emails stay in our inbox so we have a record of the conversation. If you would rather we did not keep yours, say so and we will delete it. Server records are kept by Cloudflare on their own schedule, which is a matter of days to weeks rather than years. Google Analytics data is kept for the retention period set in our Analytics account, and you can delete its cookies from your browser at any time.

Your choices

Write to us and you can ask what we hold about you, ask for a copy, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. We will do it. One email is all it takes, and we are not going to make you jump through anything.

Depending on where you live you may have these rights formally — under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California, for instance. We answer the request either way rather than checking whether we have to. We also treat a Global Privacy Control signal from your browser as a no to analytics.

Security, honestly

The site is served over HTTPS, it sends a content security policy that limits what the page is allowed to load or talk to, and the form posts straight to the form service rather than to a database we run. We are not storing a customer database, which is the best protection there is. That said, no method of sending information over the internet is completely secure, and we are not going to claim otherwise.

Links out

Where we link to somebody else's site, their privacy policy governs what they do. We have no control over it.

If this changes we will update this page and change the date at the top. If the change is a big one — adding tracking, for example — you will see it on the site, not just here.
