# The Q&A page

His eight artboards *are* the page. Nothing here redraws his artwork.

    assets/scene/Highway19-QA-artboard-01..08.svg   what he exported (untouched)
    tools/qa/make.sh                                builds everything

`make.sh` runs four steps:

1. **extract.js** — reads his artboards and writes `assets/scene/sec-01..08.svg`:
   the drawn Q&A cards, their text and their +/− markers lifted out by
   geometry; the embedded rasters externalised to `assets/img/*.webp`; his sign
   assemblies tagged `data-sign` for parallax; his wide strip elements tiled
   past the artboard on his own pitch. It also writes `cards.json` — his card
   positions, fills, strokes, type, insets, leads and marker colours.
2. **answers.py** — his questions come from his artboard. The answers he drew
   open are taken from the artboard verbatim; the rest are matched to the
   approved copy in `copy.json`.
3. **animate.js** — wraps his vehicles, ship and planes in groups the page can
   drive, and lays his own lorry onto the lanes he drew empty. `traffic.json`
   holds the lanes and the tracks.
4. **build.py** — writes `faq.html` and `assets/css/qa.css`.

Two side inputs are measured, not guessed, and are regenerated by the scripts
in the session scratchpad rather than by `make.sh`:

* `flex.json` — the two sections whose ground is provably flat between the
  cards and the CTA, so the page may open and close that band as the column
  changes height. Found by scanning every row for a colour step.
* `assets/img/bleed-*.webp` — the flanks. Each row of his own edge carried
  outward: a flat band continues exactly; a textured element his artboard cuts
  off is not smeared, the ground it stands on carries on instead.

## The rules the page keeps

* One unit is one of his artboard pixels, never more than a screen pixel, so
  his art is only ever shown at his scale or smaller.
* Answers are shut by default, one open at a time per section.
* The column never reaches his CTA panel and is never lifted off the position
  he drew it in.
* Motion is transform-only and stops for `prefers-reduced-motion`.
